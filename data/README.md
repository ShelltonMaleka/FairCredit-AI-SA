# FairCredit AI SA Dataset

## Overview

This directory contains the dataset used to develop the machine-learning component of FairCredit AI SA.

FairCredit AI SA is an explainable AI-powered credit readiness and affordability platform designed to help South African consumers better understand their financial position before applying for credit.

## Dataset

The current prototype uses a synthetic dataset containing 10,000 consumer profiles.

The dataset was created specifically for prototype development and does not contain real consumer records or personally identifiable information.

## South African Context

The dataset structure and affordability calculations were designed with reference to the South African consumer credit environment, including:

- National Credit Act 34 of 2005
- South African Affordability Assessment Regulations
- Guidance and published information from the National Credit Regulator (NCR)

Where applicable, South African affordability concepts such as income, necessary living expenses, existing credit obligations, discretionary income and proposed credit repayments are represented in the dataset.

## Important Limitation

The synthetic machine-learning target does not represent actual lending decisions or real credit outcomes from South African financial institutions.

The machine-learning component of FairCredit AI SA should therefore be considered a proof of concept.

A production version would require training, testing and independent validation using representative South African consumer credit data.

## Privacy

Synthetic data was selected because individual-level consumer credit information contains sensitive personal and financial information and representative South African credit datasets are not readily available for public machine-learning development.

Using synthetic data allows the prototype to demonstrate its technical approach without exposing real consumer financial information.

## Intended Use

The dataset is intended for:

- Machine-learning experimentation
- Credit-risk modelling demonstrations
- Explainable AI development
- Affordability analysis
- FairCredit AI SA prototype development

It should not be used to make real lending or credit decisions.
